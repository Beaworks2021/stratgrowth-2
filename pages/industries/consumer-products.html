The consumer products industry has faced a relentless wave of disruption, from a global pandemic to inflation, supply chain breakdowns, and mounting environmental pressure. Category volumes remain soft. Pricing power is hitting its limit. And consumer behavior keeps evolving, while private label and insurgent brands gain traction across the globe.

But the best consumer packaged goods companies are not standing still. They’re leveraging technology, streamlining portfolios, and reinventing their go-to-market models to outpace the competition. Our consumer products consulting expertise enables you to navigate complexity, anticipate risk, and unlock new sources of growth.

On your journey to full potential, we can help you:

  • Rethink your growth algorithm by maximizing current profit pools through superior execution and a sharper understanding of consumer shifts while making bold portfolio moves.
  • Reinvent for sustained productivity gains by simplifying today’s portfolio and operating model, strengthening supply chain resilience, and sharpening strategic focus for tomorrow.
  • Redefine an AI-led and technology-driven model by scaling value-generating digital tools today and reimagining the business for tomorrow.
  • Build sustainability into your strategy and turn a challenge into an opportunity.

Our clients include companies in food, beverage, personal and household care, consumer durables, luxury goods, apparel and footwear, recreational products, and over-the-counter medicines. The breadth of our experience and ecosystem of best-in-class partners allows us to tailor powerful solutions to your exact needs.

AI in Consumer Products

AI is helping CPGs make smarter, faster decisions across the value chain while improving both commercial execution and back-end performance.

  • Identify high-potential ingredients, products, and portfolio opportunities.
  • Fine-tune SKU mix and promotional strategy with better data.
  • Boost e-commerce performance and personalize customer engagement.
  • Accelerate content creation and improve decision making at scale.

Real Outcomes

Use AI to sharpen commercial planning, improve pricing and revenue growth management, optimize assortment decisions, and connect more effectively with consumers.
