Having mostly rebounded from its pandemic-era dip, the travel and leisure industry is poised for takeoff. New forces, including technological advances, an emphasis on personalized experiences, and increased pressure on profitability, require players to evolve to win.

We help guide travel and leisure innovators, operators, and investors, leveraging deep industry experience that includes hundreds of projects for travel agencies, online travel players, tour operators, airlines, hotels, short-term rentals, cruises, casinos, theme parks, and other location-based entertainment businesses.

Our travel and leisure consultants are immersed in the unique dynamics of each subsector and can help you better understand how to serve today’s customer, navigate emerging challenges, and build a strategy that ensures growth and sustainability.

The hospitality industry has long shown resilience to macro challenges, often finding opportunity in disruption. Covid-19, for example, reframed work expectations and blurred the line between business and leisure travel.

Now, hyper-personalization, sustainability, and technologies such as generative AI are creating new ways to deliver value. As the industry continues to face changing consumer needs and economic pressure, we partner with you to pursue smart growth now and in the future.
