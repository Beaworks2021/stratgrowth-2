The fast-changing world of retail demands adaptability. Our retail consultants know what it takes to stay out front. We pioneered the omnichannel concept a decade ago, and since then have been at the forefront of defining the future of retail.

We’re now entering the age of Retail Convergence, in which traditional incumbent retailers and digital natives compete for the same customers, talent, profit pools, and shareholders. This convergence, along with new waves of technology, proliferating consumer touchpoints, and rising sustainability expectations, increases the urgency for all players to transform their core retail and digital capabilities.

We have a proven track record of delivering measurable results, including increased sales, reduced costs, improved customer experience, impactful innovation, and higher shareholder value. Using proprietary analytics, software platforms, and deep customer insights, we help leading retailers use data, technology, and shopper trends to navigate uncertainty and leapfrog the competition.

We collaborate with you to ensure you win with all stakeholders, customers, communities, NGOs, employees, suppliers, and shareholders, while future-proofing your business through strong partnerships, expert networks, and practical execution.
