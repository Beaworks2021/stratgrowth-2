Telecommunications

Telcos face a paradox: the connectivity they provide is more important than ever to consumers and businesses alike, yet key financial and customer satisfaction measures are under pressure, forcing leaders to make hard choices about where to play and how to win.

That challenge is made more difficult by disruptive forces that will upend the traditional integrated telco model over the next decade. To respond effectively, companies need to adapt their strategies and operating models and move beyond the status quo.

Maintaining the status quo will not be an option. Companies will face focused, built-for-purpose competitors in distinct arenas including infrastructure, services, platforms, and vertical solutions. Telcos need to quickly adapt, choose their arenas wisely, and align around the rules that define success.

Strategy

Where to play and how to win

We can help you decide where to play, when to transition to a new model, and how to understand the rules that define each arena. Our telecom consulting experts have transformed the businesses of major players across the industry and have helped 93% of the world’s top 30 telecom operators.

We work closely with you to determine your ideal fighting shape, your capacity for change, your current and future technological capabilities and priorities, and, above all, your customer centricity.

We combine deep industry expertise, strategic partnerships, and proprietary tools to help you:

  • Make the right strategic decisions about wireless and wireline strategy, fixed-mobile convergence, and layer strategy.
  • Optimize infrastructure investment for the strongest return given your competitive position.
  • Simplify products and processes, reduce costs, and optimize capex to become more efficient and more effective.
  • Reimagine your operating model to be better prepared for customer and competitor actions.
  • Understand and capture M&A opportunities and best practices.
  • Integrate AI into capabilities and processes.
  • Embed environmental, social, and governance best practices across operations.

The telecommunications industry is at an inflection point. The telco of the future will embrace new technologies and processes while keeping customer needs front and center.

AI in Telecommunications

The shift from the traditional integrated telco model to infrastructure- and services-focused models is driving disruption across the industry. AI can help companies implement and thrive.

  • Advance AI-driven automation for stronger network autonomy and seamless customer experiences.
  • Optimize infrastructure to support growth and AI-driven workloads.
  • Reinvent operating models and tech stacks to boost automation, efficiency, and new revenue streams.

Our AI experts support this transition through agility, experimentation, and a renewed focus on customer needs.

Real Outcomes

Use AI to improve workforce productivity, modernize processes, reduce friction, and unlock measurable efficiency across telecom operations.
